topic: Ручные интервалы SETUP/PLANNED_STOP и effective state timeline
updated: 2026-10-08T20:15
---

- (direction) Пользователь согласовал ручное назначение SETUP/PLANNED_STOP с точными границами как overlay над calculated state, не редактирующий производную timeline.
- (capability) CAP-1: Пользователь создаёт past/current manual incident с state override SETUP или PLANNED_STOP; успех — валидный half-open interval сохраняется отдельно от calculated state.
- (capability) CAP-2: Пользователь изменяет или закрывает ongoing override; успех — границы, classification, reason и comment обновляются атомарно без пересечения другого override.
- (capability) CAP-3: Система строит effective timeline из calculated state и manual overlays; успех — manual state wins for business analytics while underlying calculated/offline state remains visible for data quality.
- (capability) CAP-4: Изменение incident актуализирует только зависимые projections; успех — dirty range covers union of old/new boundaries, raw and calculated state remain unchanged, effective intervals and aggregates republish asynchronously.
- (capability) CAP-5: Frontend может показать ручную классификацию и техническое состояние одновременно; успех — effective response exposes effectiveState, calculatedState, MANUAL/CALCULATED source, incidentId and data-quality metadata.
- (decision) Manual state override is represented by ManualIncident fields id, machineId, from, nullable to, stateOverride, nullable lossReasonId, nullable comment, createdAt and updatedAt.
- (decision) stateOverride MVP accepts only SETUP or PLANNED_STOP; RUN/IDLE/DOWN remain rule-calculated and OFFLINE remains system-calculated.
- (decision) Interval is [from,to), from required, to null means ongoing, to must be greater, from cannot precede machine coverageFrom, and future scheduling is out of scope.
- (decision) Manual state override intervals of one Machine cannot overlap; conflict is rejected rather than resolved by precedence.
- (decision) effectiveState equals manual state within override and calculatedState otherwise; when calculatedState is OFFLINE the manual business state may still apply while offline telemetry duration remains visible and counted in data quality.
- (decision) Effective projection splits at union of calculated interval and manual incident boundaries and stores both effective/calculated states, source and incident reference.
- (decision) Create/update/close endpoints mutate ManualIncident; close sets to, and update cannot change id or machineId.
- (decision) Incident mutation creates UTC-minute-aligned dirty range covering union of previous and new interval; effective timeline, state durations, shift metrics and loss analytics recalculate in background.
- (constraint) Manual overlay never updates raw telemetry or machine_state_interval and survives their replay.
- (constraint) Future manual schedules, generic incident deletion/audit and full LossReason catalog remain outside this focused block.
- (event) Coherence validation passed: five capabilities have intent and testable success; manual precedence, overlap rule, effective/calculated separation and asynchronous publication are consistent.
- (event) Preservation validation passed: accepted fields, half-open/open-ended boundaries, limited states, API, OFFLINE visibility, dirty ranges and projection algorithm are present; full generic Incident/LossReason lifecycle is explicitly out of scope.
- (event) Spec finalized after explicit user acceptance; no open questions remain in the manual state override slice.
- (direction) Позднее согласовано расширение ManualIncident: единая сущность поддерживает state override, reason annotation или оба эффекта; focused state override semantics сохраняются.
- (decision) stateOverride и lossReasonId теперь nullable, но хотя бы одно обязательно; response effectType derived, cancelledAt реализует soft cancel.
- (decision) State-effect overlaps и reason-effect overlaps проверяются независимо; state-only и reason-only incidents могут пересекаться, effective projection хранит separate stateIncidentId/reasonIncidentId/lossReasonId.
- (event) Focused manual override spec re-derived for unified ManualIncident: nullable conditional effects, separate state/reason references, soft cancel and overlap rules validated against full loss-reasons-incidents spec.
